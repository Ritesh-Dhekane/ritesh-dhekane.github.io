- **LiftLog (mobile):** weight tracking, workout logging, progress photos, analytics and history — stored on the device in SQLite with numbered migrations, no backend; whole-database backup and restore.
- Custom design system, React Native Skia charts, notifications; Android builds shared with a small group of testers.
- **LifeLogs (web):** the next step — one installable web app that grows log by log (lifts, expenses, home), storing everything on the device with optional backup to the user's own Google Drive.
