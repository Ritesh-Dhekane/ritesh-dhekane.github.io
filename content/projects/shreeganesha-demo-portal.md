- One permanent link where the client sees **every demo version**, its status (under review, approved) and what it includes.
- The first concept demo is a working jewellery storefront: product grid and categories, product details, a cart that survives a refresh, checkout validation and **WhatsApp checkout** that sends a formatted order message.
- Approvals, decisions and production releases are recorded in the repository, so the history is never lost.
- Deployed automatically to GitHub Pages on every push.
